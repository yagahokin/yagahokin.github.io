North Africa notes

Fun facts

A few useful, surprising things to know while dreaming up a route through northern Morocco and Algeria.

Mountains meet the Mediterranean

Northern Morocco is shaped by the Rif Mountains, which run close to the Mediterranean coast. It is why the scenery can shift quickly from sea views to steep, green valleys and hill towns.

A remarkable Roman coast

Algeria’s north has major Roman-era sites, including Tipasa on the Mediterranean. Its ruins sit beside the sea, blending archaeological remains with an open coastal landscape.

One important route detail

The Morocco–Algeria land and sea borders are closed, so a journey covering both countries normally needs separate flights rather than an overland crossing. Always re-check official entry and safety advice before booking.

Sources: UNESCO — Tipasa and UK FCDO travel advice.
