For Dave

Those
BEEPING MOUSE BOXES!!!

Plug-in ultrasonic rodent repellents emit high-frequency sound, typically above human hearing. They’re sometimes sold for homes as a chemical-free way to deter mice and rats.

What to know

Sound is directional: walls, cupboards, furniture, and soft furnishings create “shadows,” so one unit cannot cover an entire home.

What the evidence says

Independent guidance finds these devices have limited use for rodent control. Any initial response may fade as rodents become accustomed to the sound.

Best use

Treat one as an optional supplement—not a fix. Seal entry gaps, store food securely, remove nesting material, and use well-placed traps or a pest professional for an active infestation.

Sources: UC IPM, U.S. EPA, and FTC.
